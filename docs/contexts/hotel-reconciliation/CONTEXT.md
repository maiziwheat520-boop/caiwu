# Hotel Reconciliation

Hotel Reconciliation matches platform orders, settlement statements, withdrawals, and company
bank receipts for each hotel business.

## Language

**Platform Settlement**:
A platform-issued statement that groups hotel orders, fees, refunds, and the amount eligible for
payout.
_Avoid_: Bank receipt, gross sales

**Hotel Payout**:
A platform withdrawal expected to arrive in a company Managed Account and supported by both the
platform settlement and bank receipt.
_Avoid_: Order revenue, personal transfer

**Settlement Difference**:
The explained or unresolved difference among orders, platform deductions, refunds, and bank
receipt.
_Avoid_: Automatic write-off, rounding guess

**Company Reconciliation**:
The period report for one company that closes hotel settlements against its own evidence and
Managed Accounts.
_Avoid_: Personal reconciliation, payroll report
