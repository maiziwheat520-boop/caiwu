# Personal Finance

Personal Finance produces a complete individual financial reconciliation from consumer payment
platforms, bank statements, and linked evidence.

## Language

**Personal Statement**:
A statement for a Managed Account whose Accounting Owner is an individual.
_Avoid_: Company statement, platform summary

**Daily Consumption**:
A consumer purchase whose primary fact source is WeChat Pay or Alipay, with bank activity used
as funding evidence rather than a duplicate expense.
_Avoid_: Bank-card duplicate, internal transfer

**Large Transfer**:
A material transfer whose primary fact source is the relevant bank statement and whose
counter-account evidence determines whether it can close.
_Avoid_: Daily consumption, name-only match

**Personal Reconciliation**:
The period view that accounts for personal income, consumption, transfers, refunds, and open
evidence requests without double counting.
_Avoid_: Candidate list, attachment list
